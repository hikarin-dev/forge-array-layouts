Drawn to scale: each square is 5 × 5 tiles, and a projection lands exactly 9 tiles from its forge, so everything that stacks sits on a 9-tile lattice. An area converts 150 × chance items per minute: 30 per projection up to T3, 150 at T4.

Belt and Xiragen pipe routing are not modelled; every projected tile is assumed usable. Areas take whichever array type (L or T) the plan needs.
